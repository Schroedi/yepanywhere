# Software factories research

Research on AI agents coordinating the software development lifecycle, including the competing view of humans conducting teams of agents.

- [October 4 research collection](2026-10-04/README.md): AI Engineer uploads from September 27 through October 4, 2026, with downloaded captions, timestamped transcripts, and a source catalogue.
- [Trends and discussion](2026-10-04/trends.md): findings, disagreements, evidence limits, and questions to explore.

This is research, not an implementation plan or a change to the product roadmap.
